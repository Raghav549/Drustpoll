# Drustpoll Final UX Research Synthesis

## Evidence anchors

1. NIST SP 800-63B/63-4: phishing-resistant authenticators should use verifier-bound cryptography; WebAuthn is a standard example. This informs passkey UI and prevents presenting a fake enrollment state.
2. NIST Privacy Framework: privacy risk management should be integrated into product/service design rather than treated as a post-hoc control. This informs explicit privacy inventory, purpose, retention and audit surfaces.
3. FTC dark-pattern guidance: interfaces should not obscure, subvert or impair consumer autonomy. This informs visible consent, cancellation, advertising labels, no fake scarcity/social proof and clear destructive confirmations.
4. WCAG 2.2: pointer targets should meet minimum sizing and keyboard focus should remain visible. This informs 44px-or-better touch targets in native surfaces and visible web focus states.

## Product psychology

Use recognition before recall; progressive disclosure; consistent spatial grammar; semantic grouping; clear next actions; meaningful feedback; novelty bounded by user controls; relationship-aware context without hidden social pressure; and transparent recommendation explanations. Optimize for comprehension, confidence and agency rather than compulsive engagement.

## Visual philosophy

The Living Canvas system is intentionally distinct from Instagram: editorial asymmetry, quiet zones, custom SVG line language, semantic commerce/social accents and restrained motion. Avoid copied navigation geometry and copied visual motifs. The design system should remain legible in light, dark and high-contrast modes.

## Commerce philosophy

The profile is still the person's identity. When a storefront exists, the shop becomes a complete commerce surface inside that identity: shop identity, search, categories, collections, products, product detail, variants, delivery, address, payment, order tracking, returns, refund/exchange, support and seller policies. A user should never wonder whether they are in a social profile or a store because the transition is explicit and reversible.
